Paste a problem. Victorique asks the model service you choose for a proof, has the proof checked twice, and either delivers it or explains why it did not.

Markdown and LaTeX are kept exactly as typed and sent as plain text. Up to 256,000 bytes.

Approach方法

The two checks: a statement check asks whether the proof proves exactly the problem as stated; a proof check reads every step of the whole proof.

A check is undecided when it neither passes nor reports a serious or fatal problem, or when it returns no usable report. Used by the ladder approach.

A check can say pass and still list minor findings, such as unclear wording. Used by every approach.
